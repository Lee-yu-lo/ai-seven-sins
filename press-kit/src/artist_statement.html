作品概述（中文）

当 AI 消解了传统的道德边界，七宗罪在算法时代意味着什么？

本作品将中世纪基督教伦理中的七宗罪——饕餮、贪婪、懒惰、淫欲、骄傲、嫉妒、愤怒——投射到人工智能时代重新定义：饕餮是对 AI 生成内容的无限吞噬，贪婪是对算力与数据所有权的掠夺，懒惰是将思考外包给算法的认知怠惰，淫欲是与虚拟伴侣的情感成瘾，骄傲是人类中心主义的最后倔强，嫉妒是对 AI 能力的嫉恨，愤怒是被算法操控的群体愤怒。

作品以"关键帧图像 → 图生视频 → 人工策展剪辑 → 算法化 VJ 处理"的完整链路创作：七段 AI 生成影像各拥有独立的视觉母题与色彩系统（冷蓝/暗金/深蓝/粉紫/橙红/翠绿/血红），经人工筛选与叙事重组后，再通过五阶段 ffmpeg 像素级处理管线与程序化合成的金属质感 BGM，最终形成兼具电影叙事与 VJ 现场美学的影像装置。配套的网页端实时 VJ 混合器，使作品可在展览现场被"演奏"而非仅被"播放"。

七宗罪从未消失——它们只是等到了一个新的身体，学会了新的语言。作品试图追问：当 AI 能生成一切，人类的欲望是被放大了，还是被重新编码了？你以为你在使用 AI，还是 AI 在驯化你？

Artist Statement (English)

What do the seven deadly sins mean in the age of algorithms, as AI dissolves traditional moral boundaries?

This work reinterprets the seven deadly sins of medieval Christian ethics — Gluttony, Greed, Sloth, Lust, Pride, Envy, and Wrath — for the era of artificial intelligence. Gluttony becomes the endless consumption of AI-generated content; Greed, the plunder of compute and data; Sloth, the cognitive outsourcing of thought to algorithms; Lust, addiction to virtual companions; Pride, the last stubbornness of anthropocentrism; Envy, resentment toward machine capability; and Wrath, crowd rage engineered by recommendation systems.

The piece was created through a full human-machine pipeline: AI-generated keyframe images were transformed into video, then curated, re-edited, and processed through a five-stage pixel-level VJ pipeline with procedurally synthesized metallic sound. Each of the seven segments carries its own visual motif and color system — cold blue, dark gold, deep blue, pink-purple, orange-red, emerald, and blood red. A web-based real-time VJ mixer accompanies the work, allowing it to be performed live rather than merely played.

The seven sins never disappeared — they merely found a new body and learned a new language. The work asks: when AI can generate everything, are human desires amplified, or re-encoded? Are you using AI, or is AI domesticating you?
